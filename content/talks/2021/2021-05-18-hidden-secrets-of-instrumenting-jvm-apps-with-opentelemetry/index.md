A deep dive into instrumenting JVM-based applications with OpenTelemetry to achieve end-to-end observability. The session reveals the lesser-known details of automatic and manual instrumentation, context propagation, and exporting traces, metrics, and logs, helping developers avoid common pitfalls and gain meaningful visibility into distributed systems.

Submitting a talk to a call for papers can be intimidating, especially for first-time speakers. This interactive clinic helps prospective speakers craft strong submissions for the Elastic Community Conference, offering practical guidance on choosing a topic, writing a compelling title and abstract, and framing a proposal that stands out to reviewers.

As systems are broken into microservices, understanding what is happening across distributed calls becomes essential and traditional logging alone is no longer enough. This session walks through observability from the ground up, covering metrics, logs, and distributed tracing, and shows how to instrument microservices and correlate telemetry so engineers can quickly detect, diagnose, and resolve issues in production.

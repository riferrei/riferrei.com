Any discussion about observability inevitably turns to distributed tracing, a technology that is radically changing the way we identify and solve technical problems. Because nearly all modern applications are inherently distributed, this session gives a practical overview of the reasons behind distributed tracing, the problems it solves, its challenges, and the tools that enable a vendor-agnostic implementation, including how to choose an observability backend and the distinction between white-box and black-box instrumentation.

As organizations modernize their workloads on AWS with microservices, containers, and serverless, monitoring alone is no longer enough to understand system behavior. This session covers how to build observability into a modern AWS environment, using traces, metrics, and logs to gain end-to-end visibility across distributed and cloud-native architectures.

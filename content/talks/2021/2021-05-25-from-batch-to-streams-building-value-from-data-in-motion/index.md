Traditional batch processing forces organizations to wait for data before they can act on it, while modern businesses increasingly need to respond in real time. This talk explains the shift from batch to streaming, showing how treating data as a continuous stream in motion unlocks faster insight, event-driven architectures, and new sources of business value.

Observability starts with good instrumentation, and OpenTelemetry has become the vendor-neutral standard for producing traces, metrics, and logs. This beginner-friendly session shows Go developers how to instrument their applications with the OpenTelemetry SDK, propagate context across services, and export telemetry to the backend of their choice, without locking themselves into any single vendor.

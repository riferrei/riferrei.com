Distributed messaging systems like Apache Pulsar are notoriously hard to observe once messages flow across producers, brokers, and consumers. This session shows how to instrument Pulsar-based applications with OpenTelemetry to capture traces, metrics, and context propagation, giving developers end-to-end visibility into their streaming pipelines.

Elasticsearch is often thought of purely as a search engine, but it is also a powerful home for continuously flowing time-series and event data. This session explores data streams in Elasticsearch, showing how to ingest, index, and manage high-volume append-only data efficiently, and how features like index lifecycle management take an ingestion pipeline from merely possible to genuinely awesome.

Microservices promise agility and scalability, but the same distribution that makes them powerful can also make them fragile and hard to operate. In this playful yet practical talk, common anti-patterns and mistakes that lead teams to 'lose' their microservices are explored—from poor service boundaries and chatty communication to missing observability—along with the practices that keep distributed systems healthy and resilient.

Kafka Connect makes it easy to stream data in and out of Apache Kafka, but running it reliably at scale requires deliberate design decisions. This talk walks through the architectural considerations behind Kafka Connect deployments—standalone versus distributed mode, worker sizing and scaling, connector configuration, converters and schemas, error handling, and monitoring—so teams can build robust, maintainable data integration pipelines.

Apache Kafka has become the backbone of modern event streaming, yet many developers still have fundamental questions they never get the chance to ask. This session provides a pragmatic, no-judgment tour of Kafka's core concepts—topics, partitions, producers, consumers, offsets, and delivery guarantees—demystifying how the platform works and how to reason about it when building real applications.

Elasticsearch is far more than a search engine; it is a foundation for building responsive, data-driven applications. This session introduces the core concepts of indexing, querying, and aggregations, and demonstrates how developers can use Elasticsearch and the Elastic Stack to power features like full-text search, analytics, and real-time dashboards in their own projects.

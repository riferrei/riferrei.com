Getting data in and out of Apache Kafka correctly is one of the first challenges Go developers face. This session walks through how records are serialized and deserialized in Golang, covering the trade-offs between formats such as JSON, Avro, and Protobuf, the role of Schema Registry, and how to write producers and consumers that handle evolving schemas safely.

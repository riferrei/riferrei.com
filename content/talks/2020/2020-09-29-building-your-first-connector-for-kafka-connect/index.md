A hands-on guide to writing custom connectors for the Kafka Connect framework. The session explains the Connect architecture and the difference between source and sink connectors, then walks through implementing the connector and task APIs, handling configuration and offsets, and packaging and deploying a connector to move data reliably in and out of Apache Kafka.

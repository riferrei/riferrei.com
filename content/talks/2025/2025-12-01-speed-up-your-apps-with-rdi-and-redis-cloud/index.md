Applications often slow down because they depend on primary databases that were never designed for high-throughput, low-latency access. This talk shows how Redis Data Integration (RDI) can continuously sync data from existing databases into Redis Cloud in near real time, letting you place a fast, always-fresh caching and data layer in front of your systems to dramatically speed up application performance without rewriting your source of truth.

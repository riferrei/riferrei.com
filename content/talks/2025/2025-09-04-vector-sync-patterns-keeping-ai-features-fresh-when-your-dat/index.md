The common advice to just rebuild your vectors nightly breaks down when source data and application code change constantly, leaving AI features stale, driving up compute costs, and frustrating users. This session explores real-world synchronization patterns that work across different database ecosystems, covering event-driven architectures that update vectors in real time, smart change detection that reprocesses only what is necessary, and resilient mapping layers that insulate applications from repeated refactoring as data models evolve.

Combining a familiar voice assistant with generative AI opens up richer, more conversational experiences. This session shows how to supercharge Alexa skills using foundation models from Amazon Bedrock, connecting voice interactions to large language models so that responses become more natural, context-aware, and capable of handling open-ended questions beyond scripted intents.

An exploration of the challenges that arise when Apache Kafka becomes the system of record and schemas must evolve over time. The session covers schema management with a schema registry, compatibility strategies, and serialization formats such as Avro, Protobuf, and JSON Schema, offering practical approaches to evolve data contracts without breaking downstream consumers.

As applications grow into distributed systems of microservices, understanding how a single request travels across many components becomes essential to keeping them reliable. This session explains how distributed tracing works, how it complements metrics and logs as a pillar of observability, and how instrumenting services lets teams pinpoint latency, errors, and bottlenecks to build more reliable software.

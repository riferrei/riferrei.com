This talk explores one of Apache Kafka's most challenging concepts: determining the right number of partitions for a topic. Using a "what-if" presentation style, it examines how partition sizing affects system qualities such as consistency, concurrency, durability, and overall load capacity, highlighting the consequences of poor sizing decisions and whether recovery from them is possible.

A practical look at how Apache Kafka users can build streaming ingestion and processing pipelines on Google Cloud Platform. The session maps familiar Kafka concepts to GCP services such as Pub/Sub, Dataflow, and BigQuery, showing patterns for moving and transforming data in motion at scale.

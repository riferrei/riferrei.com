Modern event-driven applications increasingly need low-latency access to the data flowing through Apache Kafka, without hammering an external database on every request. This session shows how to keep your data close to your services by using Kafka Streams and its state stores to build local, queryable caches that stay continuously updated from your topics, turning Kafka into the backbone of fast, resilient, materialized views.

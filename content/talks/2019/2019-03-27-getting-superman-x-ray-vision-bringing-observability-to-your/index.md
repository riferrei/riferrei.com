Microservices have become the de-facto way to build complex information systems by breaking the domain problem into multiple reusable services, but this distribution makes it far harder to understand what is actually happening across a stream processing pipeline. This talk shows how to gain X-ray vision into event streaming applications by applying observability techniques such as distributed tracing, metrics, and logging so you can see how data flows through your services and pinpoint where problems occur.

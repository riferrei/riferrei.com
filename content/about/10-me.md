title: Who am I?
---

I'm a Developer Advocate, speaker, and open source contributor based in the Raleigh-Durham-Chapel Hill area of North Carolina. I work at the intersection of distributed systems, AI, and data infrastructure, helping developers build and succeed with technology.

I have more than 25 years of experience in distributed systems, databases, and software development. I began my career focused on software engineering and developer education, then moved into solution architecture, helping customers design, build, and deploy data-intensive applications. Over time I found my passion in Developer Relations, where I combine deep technical expertise, teaching experience, and customer empathy.

Along the way I've held roles at Redis, AWS, Elastic, Confluent, and Oracle.
